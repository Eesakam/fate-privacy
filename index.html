01 About HangFate

HangFate is an offline-first word puzzle game developed by Eesa Kamaldien. This policy explains how game data is used and the choices available to you.

You do not need a player account. Your saved gameplay data is stored on your device. HangFate does not provide a developer-run account or cloud-sync service for your progress.

You can play the core game offline. The initial public production release does not show ads. Some testing versions may include advertising, for which ad requests and privacy-message updates require a network connection. Other services include Android backups, sharing a result, visiting this website and contacting support.

02 Your game data

HangFate saves information locally so you can resume your puzzles and keep your progress. This includes:

  • Classic progress: your level, current puzzle, guessed letters, completion state and selected category.
  • Puzzle history: a limited record of answers you have encountered, used to reduce repeats.
  • Daily Fate: your latest saved challenge, guesses and result, plus wins and winning streaks.
  • Power-ups and rewarded ads: Lightning and All-Seeing Eye balances, earned or spent power-ups, and local transaction records used to prevent duplicate rewards and recover interrupted saves.
  • Achievements and Wardrobe: your rewards and the accessories you own and wear.
  • Preferences: settings such as whether sound effects are enabled.

These records let you continue playing, view statistics and personalise Fate. Puzzle history is not a complete lifetime record, and Daily Fate does not keep a full calendar of every past result.

03 Sharing is your choice

When you tap Share My Fate after finishing a Daily Fate puzzle, HangFate opens Android’s share sheet. The prepared text includes the challenge number and date, win or loss, disaster, remaining lives and winning streak.

HangFate does not add the puzzle answer, your name or your full gameplay history to that text. You choose whether and where to send it. The receiving app or service may store, transmit or display it, including details associated with your account on that service, under its own privacy practices.

04 Third-party services

HangFate uses Expo, React Native and related software for features such as local storage, fonts and sound. Game fonts, images and sounds are included with the app.

Some advertising-enabled testing versions of HangFate integrate Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable advertising privacy messages and choices. These advertising components are excluded from the planned initial public production build. See the next section for more information.

Storing game progress locally does not mean no technical information leaves your device. Android, Google and the other services described in this policy have their own privacy practices.

05 Advertising & privacy choices

Advertising differs by app version

Initial public production release: This version of HangFate does not display advertisements. Its advertising features are disabled, and the Google Mobile Ads and UMP native integrations are intended to be excluded from the production build.

Testing versions: Some versions available through Google Play testing tracks include Google AdMob and UMP. These versions may show a Home screen banner, interstitial advertisements at natural breaks after Classic rounds or Daily Fate, and optional rewarded advertisements. When an eligible rewarded advertisement is voluntarily watched and its reward event is confirmed, HangFate may grant a Lightning power-up under its inventory rules. Ads may not appear every time, and the core game remains playable offline.

Advertising data in enabled versions

In advertising-enabled versions, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share technical and usage data, including IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app and advertisement interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, diagnostic and performance information, and device or advertising identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID, depending on the device and applicable settings.

Google and participating advertising providers may use this information for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and other disclosed advertising purposes, subject to applicable privacy choices. Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. HangFate does not intentionally add your puzzle answers, guesses, achievements, saved progress or audio preferences to ad requests.

Consent and privacy choices

In advertising-enabled versions, HangFate uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether advertising privacy messages or choices are required. Depending on your region and applicable settings, you may see a consent message. Where offered in those versions, you can use Home → Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or update the choices presented to you. This advertising privacy option is not displayed in the initial ad-free public production release.

Refusing personalised advertising does not necessarily mean all advertising stops or that no information is processed; this depends on the available choices and applicable requirements. If an advertising permission check cannot be completed, ads may be unavailable while the game remains playable.

Future public releases may include advertising. This policy will be reviewed when advertising is enabled in production.

Learn more from Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services. Android also provides device-level advertising privacy settings.

06 Android backups & transfers

HangFate allows Android-managed backups and device transfer of ordinary local game data, including progress, achievements and preferences. This can help you keep progress when reinstalling the app or changing devices. It is managed by Android, not by a HangFate account or developer-run backup server.

If Android backs up or transfers your data, a copy may leave your device through your device’s backup or transfer service. Availability depends on Android, your device, your account, your settings and the services involved.

Restoration is controlled by Android and is not guaranteed. A restored backup may be an older snapshot, so it may not include your latest progress. Backups do not continuously sync or combine progress across devices.

Clearing app data or uninstalling HangFate does not necessarily delete existing backup copies. Manage backup and transfer options through your Android or backup-provider settings. A later restore may bring saved data back.

07 Keeping & deleting data

Game records stay in the app’s local storage until they are updated or replaced during play, removed through Android’s app-storage controls, or removed when you uninstall the app.

To delete local HangFate data, use Android’s Clear storage or Clear app data option for HangFate, or uninstall it. Device wording varies. This removes saved progress, achievements, accessories, preferences and locally stored power-up and reward records from that installation.

This does not necessarily remove Android backup copies, results shared elsewhere or information independently processed by Google and advertising providers in advertising-enabled versions. Those providers manage their own retention, deletion and privacy controls. Manage backups through Android or your backup provider, and shared results through the receiving service. There is no HangFate player account to delete.

If you contact the developer

If you email the developer, your email address, message and any attachments are handled by the developer and the email services involved. Messages are used to respond to support or privacy enquiries and related follow-up. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Correspondence may remain in email records and provider backups after a reply. This policy does not promise a fixed retention period or automatic deletion schedule. You can contact the developer to ask about access to or deletion of your correspondence.

08 This policy website

This privacy-policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, your browser contacts GitHub to load the page. GitHub may process ordinary request information such as your IP address, browser details and the time of your request.

The page itself uses only HTML and CSS. It adds no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, browser storage, external fonts or embedded third-party content. This does not prevent the hosting provider from processing requests.

For GitHub’s practices, read the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following that link or opening an email app takes you to a separate service.

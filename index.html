01 About HangFate

HangFate is an offline-first word puzzle game developed by Eesa Kamaldien. This policy explains how game data is used and the choices available to you.

You do not need a player account. Your saved gameplay data is stored on your device. HangFate does not provide a developer-run account or cloud-sync service for your progress.

Some activities involve other services, including Android backups, sharing a result, visiting this website and contacting support. These are explained below.

02 Your game data

HangFate saves information locally so you can resume your puzzles and keep your progress. This includes:

  • Classic progress: your level, current puzzle, guessed letters, completion state and selected category.
  • Puzzle history: a limited record of answers you have encountered, used to reduce repeats.
  • Daily Fate: your latest saved challenge, guesses and result, plus wins and winning streaks.
  • Achievements and Wardrobe: your rewards and the accessories you own and wear.
  • Preferences: settings such as whether sound effects are enabled.

These records let you continue playing, view statistics and personalise Fate. Puzzle history is not a complete lifetime record, and Daily Fate does not keep a full calendar of every past result.

03 Sharing is your choice

When you tap Share My Fate after finishing a Daily Fate puzzle, HangFate opens Android’s share sheet. The prepared text includes the challenge number and date, win or loss, disaster, remaining lives and winning streak.

HangFate does not add the puzzle answer, your name or your full gameplay history to that text. You choose whether and where to send it. The receiving app or service may store, transmit or display it, including details associated with your account on that service, under its own privacy practices.

04 Third-party services

HangFate uses Expo, React Native and related software to provide features such as local storage, fonts and sound. Game fonts, images and sounds are included with the app. No advertising or analytics service is configured in HangFate.

Storing game progress locally is not a promise that no information ever leaves your device or that every included software component sends no technical information. Android and third-party services have their own privacy practices. The services involved in backups, sharing, hosting and email are described in this policy.

05 Android backups & transfers

HangFate allows Android-managed backups and device transfer of ordinary local game data, including progress, achievements and preferences. This can help you keep progress when reinstalling the app or changing devices. It is managed by Android, not by a HangFate account or developer-run backup server.

If Android backs up or transfers your data, a copy may leave your device through your device’s backup or transfer service. Availability depends on Android, your device, your account, your settings and the services involved.

Restoration is controlled by Android and is not guaranteed. A restored backup may be an older snapshot, so it may not include your latest progress. Backups do not continuously sync or combine progress across devices.

Clearing app data or uninstalling HangFate does not necessarily delete existing backup copies. Manage backup and transfer options through your Android or backup-provider settings. A later restore may bring saved data back.

06 Keeping & deleting data

Game records stay in the app’s local storage until they are updated or replaced during play, removed through Android’s app-storage controls, or removed when you uninstall the app.

To delete local HangFate data, use Android’s Clear storage or Clear app data option for HangFate, or uninstall it. Device wording varies. This removes saved progress, achievements, accessories and preferences from that installation.

This does not delete Android backup copies or results you have shared elsewhere. Manage backups through Android or your backup provider, and shared results through the receiving service. There is no HangFate player account to delete.

If you contact the developer

If you email the developer, your email address, message and any attachments are handled by the developer and the email services involved. Messages are used to respond to support or privacy enquiries and related follow-up. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Correspondence may remain in email records and provider backups after a reply. This policy does not promise a fixed retention period or automatic deletion schedule. You can contact the developer to ask about access to or deletion of your correspondence.

07 This policy website

This privacy-policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, your browser contacts GitHub to load the page. GitHub may process ordinary request information such as your IP address, browser details and the time of your request.

The page itself uses only HTML and CSS. It adds no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, browser storage, external fonts or embedded third-party content. This does not prevent the hosting provider from processing requests.

For GitHub’s practices, read the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following that link or opening an email app takes you to a separate service.

08 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when HangFate’s features or data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Please check it for the latest information.
